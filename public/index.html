The approach

No slide deck.
Decisions.

Founders reach out when a technical decision blocks everything else — choosing a stack, deciding between refactor and patch, hiring the right person, getting a team moving again.

My role: diagnose the situation, make the call with you, structure what comes next. I don't produce 40-page reports. I unblock what's blocked.

"The real cost of a bad tech decision isn't what it takes to fix it. It's everything you couldn't build while you were hesitating."

Formats

How
I work

Three formats, depending on what you need — from a one-off decision to ongoing co-piloting.

🔍

Strategic audit

Clarify the situation, identify the real blockers, and leave with clear priorities and an actionable roadmap. One-off engagement, concrete deliverable.

🧭

Ongoing support

One or two days a week to co-pilot decisions over time. Hiring, architecture, tooling choices, calls made with the team.

⚡

Targeted intervention

A tech hire to validate, a vendor to assess, an architecture decision to settle. One day, one topic, one outcome.
